# **Our Programs** 

We offer a progressive pathway designed for the early and formative years: 

- **Kindergarten & Daycare** – A warm, caring start for our youngest learners, focusing on playbased discovery, social skills, and foundational literacy and numeracy. 

- **Preschool / Playgroup** – Structured yet playful learning that builds readiness for primary school through language, numbers, creativity, and emotional growth. 

- **Lower Primary (Grade 1 – Grade 4)** – A solid academic foundation aligned with national curriculum standards, combined with values education and hands-on learning experiences. 

# **Co-Curricular Activities** 

As part of our commitment to a diverse and enriching learning experience, we offer a range of exciting activities that develop physical skills, coordination, confidence, and creativity: 

- Swimming

- Gymnastics

- Music and Creative Arts
